# 2026-11-17 — IDE: disk images over ATA, PIO and DMA

- `BlockDevice` interface (512-byte sectors) plus `RamBlockDevice`. The
  SD-card image backend on the Pi comes later.
- `AtaBus`: an ATA channel with two devices, following ATA/ATAPI-5/6:
  IDENTIFY, PIO and multiple-mode reads and writes, DMA, CHS and LBA28,
  error handling, SRST, nIEN and diagnostics.
- `MacioIde`: the mac-io ATA cell (register layout per Linux pata_macio)
  joined to a DBDMA channel; IDE0 and IDE1 are mapped in Paddington with
  QEMU's Old World offsets, channels and IRQs (to verify).
- While researching this, found that DBDMA channels are 0x80 apart, not
  0x100 (that value had no source), and fixed it in a separate commit.
- Sabotage: 13 mutations. Three first went unnoticed (selection not
  updating INTRQ, an interrupt in the middle of a DMA transfer, 32-bit PIO
  write order), so the tests were tightened and all are caught now. One
  mutation (dropping the CHS sector-0 check) gives identical behaviour,
  since the address then wraps out of range and IDNF follows anyway.
- 170/170 tests pass.
