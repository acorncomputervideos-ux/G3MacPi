## Hardware test 1 — Raspberry Pi 400 (owner, build 4f01c63)

**Result: boots.** HDMI showed the banner and the full log:
- `Circle 51.1.1 started on Raspberry Pi 400 4GB (AArch64)`, revision code `c03130`, GCC 15.2.1
- `Host RAM: 4096 MB, Circle heap: 2995 MB`
- `Screen: 1280x1024`
- **`Core self-test: PASSED`** — the portable core gives correct results on the Cortex-A72.

Problems found and fixed in the next commit:
1. **`ARM clock: 600 MHz`** — Circle leaves the cores at minimum clock unless
   asked. Now uses `CCPUThrottle(CPUSpeedMaximum)`; the log shows current/max
   clock and SoC temperature. All future benchmarks depend on this.
   Note for later: the emulator loop must call `CCPUThrottle::Update()` every
   few seconds; Circle then throttles at `socmaxtemp` (default 60 °C, settable
   in `cmdline.txt`).
2. Banner text was red, not white: Circle draws bold (`ESC[1m`) in
   `HIGH_COLOR` = bright red. Bold removed.
3. Black notch in the banner: banner lines had different lengths. All lines
   are now padded to 64 columns.

Not yet re-tested on hardware after these fixes.
