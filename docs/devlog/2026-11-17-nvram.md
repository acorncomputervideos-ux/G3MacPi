# 2026-11-17 — mac-io NVRAM

- `MacioNvram`: 8 KB, direct access with a 16-byte stride, mapped at mac-io
  BAR + 0x60000 (QEMU Old World layout; B&W placement and style to verify).
  The Linux driver's two other styles (indirect and flash) are documented
  in case the device tree shows otherwise.
- Persistence hooks (`data`, `load`, `dirty`) for saving to the SD card
  later.
- Sabotage: lane order, the dirty flag, reads that straddle cells, load()
  not clearing dirty, and Paddington not unmapping the NVRAM are all caught.
  The straddling-read case first went unnoticed, so a check was added.
- 157/157 tests pass.
